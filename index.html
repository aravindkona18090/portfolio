CivicConnect — Smart Municipal Intelligence & Civic Redressal Platform

An end-to-end smart city governance platform connecting citizens, municipal field officers, and administrators — leveraging Multimodal Vision AI, 45m Haversine GIS deduplication, and verified Before-vs-After resolution auditing.

  • Google Gemini Multimodal Vision AI: Auto-analyzes uploaded photo evidence to detect category (Roads, Sanitation, Electricity, Water), rate severity levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low), and generate issue descriptions (with PyTorch MobileNetV2 offline fallback).
  • 3-Tier Role Architecture (Citizen, Field Worker, Admin): Citizens get AI camera scanning, voice-to-text, & 24/7 CivicBot AI chat (4 languages: EN, TE, HI, KN); Field Workers get turn-by-turn Google Maps GPS navigation & 1-click translation; Admins get severity heatmaps & proof-of-fix verification.
  • 45-Meter GIS Haversine Deduplication: Eliminates municipal ticket spam by detecting duplicate reports within a 45m radius on Leaflet.js / OpenStreetMap before worker allocation.
  • Verified Before-vs-After Audit & Security: Enforces photographic proof comparison before closing tickets, built on PHP 8 REST APIs, BCRYPT hashing, 100% prepared SQL statements, and environment variable API key protection (getenv('GEMINI_API_KEY')).
